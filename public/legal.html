Judge and court lookup MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT

Add one URL to your AI assistant and it can find a judge by name and state, pull a judge's court, caseload and practice-area activity, list recent public cases, and search courts. Results come from JudgeFinder and say where they came from.

Get a free keyHow to connect

The five tools

legal_search_judgesquery, state, limit.
legal_get_judgeslug. Court, caseload, practice-area activity.
legal_judge_recent_casesjudge_id, limit.
legal_search_courtsquery, state, county, type, limit.
legal_court_judgescourt_id, limit.

Example

Ask your assistant: Find judges named Rivera in California, then show the recent cases for the first one. It calls the tools in order:

legal_search_judges
  query: "Rivera"
  state: "CA"

legal_judge_recent_cases
  judge_id: (id from the first result)
  limit: 10

Where the data comes from

The tools read from JudgeFinder, a judge lookup built on CourtListener docket metadata, built by the same operator. Records can be incomplete or out of date, so each answer names its source and the assistant can send you there to check.

Read this before you use it

The results are public-record information. They are not legal advice, and they are not a basis for deciding anything about a case on their own. Verify against the court's own records.

Connect it

Server URL: https://thefiredev.com/mcp. Claude and ChatGPT walk you through sign-in, where you paste a key or create a free one. A free key allows 100 tool calls a day with no card. Steps for each client are on the connect section and in the docs. The same server also has EMS protocol tools, public health data and open-model sizing.
